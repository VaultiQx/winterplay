How to play

Three steps, the same for every game on the hub.

1

Pick a game

Choose any of the four games above. Each one opens and plays right here in your browser.

2

Play and score

Every game uses a few keys, listed on its card. Chase coins, dodge what is in the way, and push your score up.

3

Beat your best

Chase your best score in every game, unlock its five achievements as you go, and come back to top them.

About the hub

One small arcade on the ice. Every game stars Lofi, runs in the browser, and takes a minute to learn.

Free to play

Every game is free. No downloads, no installs, no payments. Just open a game and play.

Sign in, your way

Sign in with Google, email or your SUI Wallet, no seed phrase ever asked. Or play as a guest and link an account later, your progress comes with you.

Four ways to play

Run, cross, slide and climb. Four different mini games, one yeti, all set on the same snowy mountain.

Meet Lofi

Lofi lives in an ice cave halfway up the mountain. Fluffy white fur, big shiny eyes, rosy cheeks, a tiny pink nose and the sweetest smile on the mountain. Every game on this hub is one of his adventures on the ice.
